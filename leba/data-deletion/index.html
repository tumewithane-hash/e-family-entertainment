Data deletion

Draft instructions; manual deletion workflow and retention must be verified by the operator.

Disconnect

Sign in to LEBA, open your company, find the connected account and select Disconnect. You can also revoke authorization through the social platform.

Request deletion

Email tumewithane@gmail.com with the subject LEBA Data Deletion Request. Identify your LEBA account email and relevant company or profile. Never send passwords, tokens or identity documents in the initial request.

Next steps

The operator will verify the request and explain deletion and any necessary retention. Backup retention and response timelines require confirmation.
