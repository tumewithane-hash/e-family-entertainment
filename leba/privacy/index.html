Privacy Policy

Operator: E-Family Entertainment · Draft updated 24 November 2026. Subject to operator review before submission.

Information handled

LEBA may process registration and sign-in information, company and connected profile details, platform account identifiers, display names, follower counts and available analytics, authorization tokens and operational logs.

Use of information

Information is used to authenticate users, connect requested accounts, retrieve and display analytics, maintain history and authorized connections, secure and troubleshoot the service and respond to requests. Social platform authorization takes place with the platform.

Platform access and sharing

LEBA exchanges information with supported social platforms as authorized. Infrastructure providers may process information to operate the service. Disclosure may also be necessary for legal obligations or security.

Retention and security

Account records and analytics may remain until removal or fulfillment of a deletion request, subject to necessary legal, security and backup retention. Exact backup retention requires operator verification. We aim to restrict access to credentials and tokens; no online service guarantees absolute security.

Your choices

Disconnect accounts in LEBA or revoke authorization through platform settings. Request access, correction or deletion by contacting tumewithane@gmail.com. See data deletion instructions.

Eligibility and changes

LEBA is intended for users eligible to manage the accounts they connect. This policy may be updated as practices or requirements change.
